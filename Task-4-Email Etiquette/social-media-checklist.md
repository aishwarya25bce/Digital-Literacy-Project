## Social Media Do’s and Don’ts for Students

### ✅ Do’s
- Maintain a professional and appropriate profile
- Share informative and positive content
- Use strong privacy and security settings
- Think before posting anything online
- Respect others’ opinions and communicate politely

### ❌ Don’ts
- Do not share personal or sensitive information
- Do not post offensive or inappropriate content
- Do not spread misinformation or unverified news
- Do not engage in cyberbullying or arguments
- Do not overshare private details online
