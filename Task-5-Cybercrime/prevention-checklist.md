## Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Do not share OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone  
- Always verify payment requests before approving them  
- Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts  
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)  
- Avoid clicking on unknown or suspicious links  

### 💰 UPI / Payment Safety
- Never approve a UPI request unless you initiated it  
- Do not scan unknown QR codes for receiving money  
- Verify the sender before making any transaction  

### 📢 Reporting Cybercrime
- National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in  
- Helpline Number: 1930
